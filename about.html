Safety Map

Our website is a safety map.

It shows small and important events around you.

Users can report potholes, crashes, road blocks, broken streetlights, and other dangerous situations.

The reports will appear on the map.

People can use the map to see what is happening nearby.

When an event is over, users can report that it has ended. If several people confirm that it is over, the report will disappear from the map.

Our goal is to give people quick and useful safety information about what is happening around them.
